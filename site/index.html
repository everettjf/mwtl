Win32 stays visible

Retrieve the HWND at any time and call SendMessageW, PostMessageW, or other Windows APIs directly.

Real C++17

The public target explicitly requires cxx_std_17 and does not depend on concepts, modules, or standard coroutines.

Reproducible dependencies

WTL and WIL come from their official repositories at immutable commit SHAs, with offline source overrides supported.
